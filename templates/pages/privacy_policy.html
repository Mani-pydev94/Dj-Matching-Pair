Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Campus Connect AI helps students find study partners and connect with their campus community. This policy explains what information the service handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide or create while using the service, including:

  • Account details such as your name, email address, and password (stored in a protected, hashed form).
  • Profile details you choose to add, such as your university, major, year, age, city, gender, languages, biography, interests, and profile photo.
  • Your questionnaire answers and preferences, which are used to calculate compatibility and prepare your match experience.
  • Messages and images you send to other users, connection requests, community memberships, and notification state.
  • Event registration details. Depending on the event form, this may include your name, email address, and phone number.

The service also uses essential session and security data to keep you signed in and protect forms. Your browser may request fonts from Google Fonts when pages load; Google may receive standard connection information for those requests.

How we use information

We use information to provide and maintain your account, verify your email, display your profile, calculate and show potential matches, deliver messages and notifications, administer communities and events, and protect the service from misuse.

Matching and profile visibility

Profile details and questionnaire responses help determine compatibility. Your profile visibility setting controls whether your profile can be discovered. Some profile details may be shown to other members as part of matching or after a connection is accepted. Information you send in a message or submit for an event is available to its intended recipient or event organizers as needed to provide that feature. Please take care when sharing information with other members.

Optional AI features

If AI-generated questionnaire reflections are enabled, your questionnaire answers are sent to Groq only after you give separate consent to that feature. This processing is optional and is not required to use the questionnaire or matching service. Groq processes that information under its own terms and privacy practices; review those before enabling the feature.

When information is shared

We do not describe profile data as public by default; visibility depends on your settings and the feature you use. Information may be shared:

  • With other members when you use profiles, matching, connections, communities, or messaging.
  • With event organizers when you register for an event.
  • With service providers that support hosting, email delivery, or an optional feature you choose, only as needed for those services.
  • When required by law or needed to protect users, the service, or others from harm or misuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and account choices

We keep information while it is needed to provide the service and meet legitimate operational or legal needs. You can update profile information and visibility through your account. To ask for account or data deletion, contact the service operator through the contact channel provided by your institution or service operator. Some information may remain in backups or records where retention is required or reasonably necessary.

Security and age

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. This service is designed for students; if you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact the service operator through your institution or service operator's contact channel.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date at the top indicates when it was most recently updated. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact channel provided by your institution or service operator.
